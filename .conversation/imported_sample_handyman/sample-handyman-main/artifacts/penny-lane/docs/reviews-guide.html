How Reviews Work — Owner's Guide

Penny Lane Home Solutions · pennylanehomesolutions.com · Last updated August 2026

This guide explains everything that happens with customer reviews on your website — from the moment a customer clicks your review link to their words appearing on your homepage. No technical knowledge needed.

The one rule to remember: nothing a customer writes ever appears on your website without your approval.

  1. You mark a job Completed → customer gets a thank-you email with the review link
  2. Customer submits a review on the website
  3. The review waits as Pending — invisible to the public
  4. You Approve it in Admin → Reviews
  5. It appears in "What Clients Say" on your homepage

1How customers reach the review form

Customers get to the review form through one link:

pennylanehomesolutions.com/#testimonials

It scrolls straight to the "Worked with Brandon?" form on your homepage — on phones and computers alike. Customers receive it:

  • Automatically: when you mark a job Completed in Admin → Bookings, the customer gets a thank-you email with a green "Leave us a review" button. (Sent only once per job, and only if their email is on the booking.)
  • Manually: any time you text or email the link yourself.
  • On their own: via the "Reviews" link in the website menu.
ExampleYou finish Sarah's faucet repair and tap "Completed" on her booking. Within a minute, Sarah has a friendly email thanking her, with a button that takes her right to the review form.

2The customer submits a review

The form asks for their name, a star rating (1–5), their review text, and optionally their city and the service used.

Built-in protections:

  • The review must be at least a sentence long; names must look like real names.
  • Spam limit: each visitor can submit at most 3 reviews per 10 minutes. Anyone flooding the form sees "Too many reviews submitted. Please try again later."

3The review is saved — but NOT public

Every submission is stored safely as Pending. It appears nowhere on the website yet. The customer sees a thank-you message saying you'll feature their review soon.

Example — spamSomeone submits "BEST PRICES call 555-…" with a link. It just sits in your Pending list where only you can see it. Never approve it — or delete it — and it never touches your website.

4Glowing reviews get a Google nudge

Right after submitting, an AI helper reads the review (takes a second or two, never delays the customer):

  • Excellent review (glowing, genuine — usually 5 stars): the thank-you screen shows a prominent "Post it on Google" button. One tap copies their words and opens Google's review page — they just paste and post. Their review counts twice: on your site AND on Google, where new customers search.
  • Ordinary review (fine but not glowing, complaints, or anything odd): the normal polite thank-you, with only a small "share on Google" text link. That link shows for everyone, which keeps things fair under Google's rules.
ExampleSarah writes 5 stars: "Brandon fixed our leaking outdoor faucet the same week we called. Professional and fair pricing." → She's invited to post it on Google too.

Dave writes 3 stars: "Job was fine." → Just the standard thank-you.
Good to knowIf the AI is ever unavailable, a simple backup rule takes over (5 stars + a substantive review = excellent), so the form always works.

5You approve reviews in Admin

Go to Admin → Reviews. You'll see three sections:

SectionWhat it meansWhat you can do
PendingNew reviews awaiting your decisionApprove (publishes it) or Delete (gone forever)
PublishedLive on the homepage right nowRemove (hides it, keeps it saved) or Delete
From GoogleYour Google reviews, synced automatically every morningRead-only — managed on Google
  • Approve → the review appears on the homepage immediately; you'll see a "Review published" confirmation.
  • Remove → hides it from the site but keeps it, so you can re-approve later.
  • Delete → permanent (you'll be asked to confirm).

6Where reviews appear

Approved reviews show in the "What Clients Say" section of your homepage, in the swipeable card carousel, mixed with your synced Google reviews (marked with a Google badge). Google reviews refresh automatically once a day.

Frequently asked questions

A customer says they left a review but I don't see it on the site.
That's by design — check Admin → Reviews → Pending and approve it.
I approved a review. How fast is it live?
Instantly. Refresh the homepage and it's there.
Can I edit a customer's review?
No — reviews appear exactly as written (that keeps them trustworthy). If something's off, Remove or Delete it instead.
A customer got a "too many reviews" message.
They tried submitting more than 3 times in 10 minutes. Ask them to wait 10 minutes and try once more.
A customer didn't get the thank-you email after I completed their job.
The email needs their address on the booking — check the booking has an email. It's also only sent the first time a job is marked Completed.
Do I have to do anything for Google reviews?
No. They sync automatically each morning and appear alongside site reviews. To get more of them, keep sharing the review link — the site nudges your happiest customers toward Google for you.
