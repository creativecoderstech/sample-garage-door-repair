# How Reviews Work — Owner's Guide

*Sample Handyman · sample-handyman.com · Last updated August 2026*

This guide explains everything that happens with customer reviews on your website — from the moment a customer clicks your review link to their words appearing on your homepage. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## The big picture

```
Job completed → Thank-you email with review link → Customer submits review
      → Review waits as "Pending" → You approve it in Admin → It's live on the homepage
```

Nothing a customer writes ever appears on your website without your approval.

---

## Step 1 — How customers reach the review form

Customers get to the review form through one link:

**https://sample-handyman.com/#testimonials**

This link scrolls straight to the **"Worked with Mike?"** form on your homepage (on both phones and computers). Customers receive it:

- **Automatically:** when you mark a job **Completed** in Admin → Bookings, the customer gets a thank-you email with a green **"Leave us a review"** button pointing here. (Sent only once per job, and only if you have their email on file.)
- **Manually:** any time you text or email the link yourself.
- **On their own:** by clicking "Reviews" in the website menu.

> **Example:** You finish Sarah's faucet repair and tap "Completed" on her booking. Within a minute, Sarah has a friendly email thanking her, with a button that takes her right to the review form.

## Step 2 — The customer submits a review

The form asks for their **name**, a **star rating (1–5)**, their **review text**, and optionally their city and the service used.

Built-in protections:

- The review must be at least a sentence long; names must look like real names.
- **Spam limit:** each visitor can submit at most **3 reviews per 10 minutes**. Someone trying to flood the form sees "Too many reviews submitted. Please try again later."

## Step 3 — The review is saved, but NOT public

Every submission is stored safely as **Pending**. It does not appear anywhere on the website yet. The customer sees a thank-you message saying you'll feature their review soon.

> **Example — spam:** Someone submits "BEST PRICES call 555-…" with a link. It just sits in your Pending list where only you can see it. Never approve it — or delete it — and it never touches your website.

## Step 4 — Glowing reviews get a Google nudge

Right after submitting, an AI helper reads the review (this takes a second or two and never delays the customer):

- **Excellent review** (glowing, genuine — usually 5 stars): the thank-you screen shows a prominent **"Post it on Google"** button. One tap copies their words and opens Google's review page so they can paste and post. Their review counts twice — on your site AND on Google, where new customers search.
- **Ordinary review** (fine but not glowing, complaints, or anything odd): they get the normal polite thank-you, with only a small "share on Google" text link (that link is shown to everyone, which keeps things fair under Google's rules).

> **Example:** Sarah writes 5 stars: "Mike fixed our leaking outdoor faucet the same week we called. Professional and fair pricing." → She's invited to post it on Google too.
> Dave writes 3 stars: "Job was fine." → Just the standard thank-you.

If the AI is ever unavailable, a simple backup rule takes over (5 stars + a substantive review = excellent), so the form always works.

## Step 5 — You approve reviews in Admin

Go to **Admin → Reviews**. You'll see three sections:

| Section | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | New reviews awaiting your decision | **Approve** (publishes it) or **Delete** (gone forever) |
| **Published** | Live on the homepage right now | **Remove** (hides it, keeps it saved) or **Delete** |
| **From Google** | Your Google reviews, synced automatically every morning | Read-only — managed on Google |

- **Approve** → the review appears on the homepage immediately; you'll see a "Review published" confirmation.
- **Remove** → hides it from the site but keeps it, so you can re-approve later.
- **Delete** → permanent (you'll be asked to confirm).

## Step 6 — Where reviews appear

Approved reviews show in the **"What Clients Say"** section of your homepage, in the swipeable card carousel, mixed with your synced Google reviews (marked with a Google badge). Google reviews refresh automatically once a day.

## Frequently asked questions

**A customer says they left a review but I don't see it on the site.**
That's by design — check **Admin → Reviews → Pending** and approve it.

**I approved a review. How fast is it live?**
Instantly. Refresh the homepage and it's there.

**Can I edit a customer's review?**
No — reviews are shown exactly as written (that keeps them trustworthy). If something's off, Remove or Delete it instead.

**A customer got a "too many reviews" message.**
They tried submitting more than 3 times in 10 minutes. Ask them to wait 10 minutes and try once more.

**A customer didn't get the thank-you email after I completed their job.**
The email needs their address on the booking — check the booking has an email. It's also only sent the first time a job is marked Completed.

**Do I have to do anything for Google reviews?**
No. They sync automatically each morning and appear alongside site reviews. To get *more* of them, keep sharing the review link — the site nudges your happiest customers toward Google for you.
